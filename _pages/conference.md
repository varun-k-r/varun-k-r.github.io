<p class="page-intro">New Perspectives on the Political Economy of Development</p>

<p>I co-convene the annual conference &quot;New Perspectives on the Political Economy of Development&quot; with <a href="https://zuheirdesai.com/">Zuheir Desai</a>. This conference focuses on the latest research in the Political Economy of Development (broadly defined). Our goal is to bring together prominent political economy scholars specializing in the study of development and the developing world.</p>

## 2026

We extend our sincere gratitude to the Department of Political Science at Ohio State University, the Center for International Studies and the Department of Political Science and International Relations at the University of Southern California for their invaluable support. We also thank our esteemed presenters, discussants, and keynote speaker: <a href="https://aviditacharya.humsci.stanford.edu/">Avidit Acharya</a> (Stanford), <a href="https://www.caterinachiopris.com/">Caterina Chiopris</a> (Columbia), <a href="https://www.irfannooruddin.org/">Irfan Nooruddin</a> (Georgetown), <a href="https://jpierskalla.github.io/">Jan Pierskalla</a> (OSU), <a href="https://sites.google.com/site/jessicaagottlieb/">Jessica Gottlieb</a> (University of Houston), <a href="https://imai.fas.harvard.edu/">Kosuke Imai</a> (Harvard), and <a href="https://soerenhenn.com/">Soeren Henn</a> (UW-Madison).</p>
